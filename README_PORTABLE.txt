VIDEO DOWNLOADER - BAN PORTABLE

1. Giai nen toan bo file ZIP vao mot thu muc co quyen ghi, vi du Desktop hoac Documents.
2. Bam dup VideoDownloader.exe. Giao dien se tu mo trong Google Chrome.
3. Chon thu muc cha, dan danh sach link va bam "Tai tat ca".

Khong can cai Python hay FFmpeg. FFmpeg da duoc dong goi trong file EXE.

Du lieu rieng cua may dong goi KHONG nam trong goi nay:
- Khong kem cookie dang nhap.
- Khong kem lich su tai.
- Khong kem video da tai.
